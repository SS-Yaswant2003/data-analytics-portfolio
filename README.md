# 📊 Data Analytics Portfolio

A collection of data analytics projects developed using **Microsoft Excel and Power BI**, focusing on data analysis, visualization, dashboard development, KPI reporting, and business insights.

---

## 📁 Projects

### 1. 📊 Sales Analysis Dashboard — Microsoft Excel

An interactive sales analytics project developed in Microsoft Excel to analyze sales performance across regions, product categories, sales representatives, months, and states.

#### 🎯 Project Objective

The objective of this project was to transform raw sales data into an interactive dashboard that helps identify sales trends, regional performance, product performance, salesperson performance, and profitability.

#### 📌 Dataset

The dataset contains **500 sales transactions** with information including:

* Order ID
* Order Date
* Region
* State
* Salesperson
* Customer
* Product Category
* Product
* Quantity
* Unit Price
* Discount
* Sales
* Cost
* Profit
* Payment Mode

#### 🛠️ Tools & Techniques

* Microsoft Excel
* Data organization and cleaning
* PivotTables
* PivotCharts
* Slicers
* Dashboard creation
* Sales and profit analysis
* Regional analysis
* Product-category analysis
* Monthly trend analysis

#### 📈 Key Metrics

| Metric       |         Value |
| ------------ | ------------: |
| Total Sales  |   ₹27,267,000 |
| Total Profit | ₹6,322,873.50 |
| Units Sold   |         1,033 |
| Transactions |           500 |

#### 🔍 Key Insights

* **East** was the highest-performing region with sales of approximately **₹7.57 million**.
* **Electronics** generated the highest sales among product categories, contributing approximately **₹19.56 million**.
* **Rahul** was the highest-performing salesperson with approximately **₹5.40 million** in sales.
* **January** recorded the highest monthly sales at approximately **₹3.53 million**.
* **April** recorded the lowest monthly sales at approximately **₹1.06 million**.
* The dashboard provides regional and product-level views of both sales and profitability.

### 2. 📱 Mobile Sales Dashboard — Power BI

An interactive mobile sales analytics dashboard developed using Microsoft Power BI to analyze sales performance, customer behavior, brands, mobile models, payment methods, and geographic trends.

#### 🎯 Project Objective

The objective of this project was to build an interactive Power BI dashboard that provides a comprehensive view of mobile sales performance and enables users to explore the data through filters and interactive visualizations.

#### 📊 Dashboard Pages

The Power BI report contains five analytical pages:

**1. Dashboard**

* Total Sales
* Total Units
* Total Transactions
* Average Price
* Average Customer Rating
* Monthly sales trend
* Sales by brand
* Geographic analysis
* Top 10 mobile models by sales
* Payment method analysis
* Interactive filters

**2. Sales Analysis**

* Monthly sales trend
* Sales KPIs
* Monthly units sold
* Brand and unit-sales analysis
* Date-based filtering

**3. Brand & Model Analysis**

* Total sales by brand
* Units sold by brand
* Top 10 mobile models by total sales
* Average customer rating by brand

**4. Customer Analysis**

* Transactions by age group
* Payment method distribution
* Customer ratings by payment method
* Sales by age group
* Customer-related filtering

**5. City Analysis**

* Geographic sales analysis
* Sales by city
* Top cities based on price per unit
* Average customer rating by city
* City-based filtering
